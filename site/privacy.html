Privacy

Last changed 2 November 2026.

What is stored

Your email address, so you can sign in. Your journal: dishes, recipe versions, cook logs, photos you add, your fridge items, your shopping list, and the conversation you have with the assistant. All of it belongs to your account.

What is sent to a model

A reel link and its caption when you import, so the recipe can be read out of it. A receipt photo when you ask for one to be read: the photo is sent once, the lines come back for you to confirm, and the photo itself is not stored. Questions you ask the assistant, with the recipe they are about.

What the Wall shows

The Wall lists imported recipes with their thumbnail and the original creator. It never shows who imported them, and the database keeps no such link.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third party trackers. This page sets no cookies.

Removing your data

Reset journal, in the You tab, wipes the local copy. For the account itself, open an issue on the repository and it is deleted by hand.
